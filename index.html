Help

How do I make a worksheet?

On Home, choose Exam and Class, tap a subject, then a chapter. Tick the questions you want. Tap the round Worksheet button at the bottom, check the preview, and tap Download PDF.

Can I take questions from many chapters?

Yes. Your ticks stay when you go back. Open another chapter and tick more. The Worksheet button shows the total.

Where is my PDF saved?

In your phone's Downloads folder. Every downloaded worksheet is also kept in My Worksheets, so you can download it again any time.

How do I add my logo and academy name?

Open the menu, then Profile & Branding. Your details appear on every worksheet header and footer.

How do I renew my plan?

Call or WhatsApp us from the menu. Your plan is extended from the day it ends, so no days are lost.
